What moves total cost C (from the published formula; the C of every standing term sheet is computed for you):
- **effective_date**: units bought before the new price applies are charged at the current price. A later start of a price increase saves (new - current) × units before it.
- **payment_days**: paying later than the reference saves spend × interest × days / 360 (8 % a year: 30 days ≈ 0.67 % of spend). A cash-short supplier values fast payment far more than that: trade shorter payment for a larger price cut.
- **contract_months / volume_per_year**: need not covered by the deal costs the fallback price plus the fallback risk. Cover the full need over the horizon. Contract months beyond the horizon cost nothing and give the supplier security: a cheap give. Volume above the need costs money.
- **incoterm**: freight per unit by Incoterm from the case file. Compare it with the price difference.
- **lead_time_days**: every day beyond the needed lead time costs the case's late cost per day.
- **ppm_commitment / chargeback_eur_per_complaint**: expected defects × (cost per defect − chargeback). Matters most in quality_first cases.
- **index_clause**: always costs the index risk % on the indexed share, plus the expected index move. Accept one only if the index path falls clearly.
- **stabilise / secure_supply**: squeezing the supplier below a sustainable margin is charged. Do not push a strategic supplier to its floor; trade terms instead.
- No deal scores the alternative. A broken must-have scores 0: never accept one.
